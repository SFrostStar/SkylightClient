A modern, high-performance **CS-Style ClickGUI Script** for Roblox executors, featuring combat modules, movement enhancements, atmosphere customizers, and real-time HUD widgets.

---

## 🚀 Quick Execution (One-Line Loader)

Copy and paste the following line into your executor:

```lua
loadstring(game:HttpGet('https://raw.githubusercontent.com/SFrostStar/SkylightClient/main/main.lua'))()
```

## ✨ Features

### ⚔️ Combat
- **Aura (KillAura)**: Silent server-side rotation with smooth ~10 CPS attack rate and 0.2s jump critical attack timing.
- **AimLock**: Sticky target acquisition locked to target's torso (Default bind: `C`).
- **Team Check**: Ignores teammates in team-based games.

### ⚡ Movement
- **Speed**: CFrame movement multiplier in Hold Mode (Default bind: `LShift`).
- **VClip UP & DOWN**: Vertical teleportation with zero gravity Y-velocity accumulation and continuous WASD momentum.

### 👁️ Visuals & Atmosphere
- **ESP (3D Glow)**: Always-on-top player highlight.
- **Tracers**: Clean screen-to-player tracer lines.
- **3D Box**: 3D bounding box surrounding players.
- **AimLock FOV Circle**: Adjustable capture radius circle overlay around cursor.
- **TargetESP**: Sci-Fi dual-ring counter-rotating reticle with pulsing center ring locked on target.
- **Purple Ambiance**: Custom screen tint and atmospheric lighting.
- **Custom Purple Fog**: Adjustable fog color and fog density slider.
- **Night / Galaxy Sky**: Custom starry skybox with midnight atmosphere.

### 👤 Player
- **Infinite Jump**: Jump freely in mid-air.
- **Camera FOV Customizer**: Dynamic field-of-view slider.

### ⌨️ HUD Widgets & Permanent Watermark
- **Hotkeys HUD Widget**: Floating CS-style widget displaying active keybinds and statuses (`ON`, `OFF`, `HOLD`).
- **TargetHUD Widget**: Real-time avatar headshot, player name, HP text, and smooth animated health bar.
- **Permanent Top-Left Watermark**: Clean auto-fitting badge (`Skylight Client | By A1wertykss | Discord: xsynapse | v2.0`).

| :--- | :--- | :--- |
